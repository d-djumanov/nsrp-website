What we handle

Your registration, kept current.

Extension of validity

Registration certificates for medicines and medical devices are valid for five years. We track your certificate's expiry and tell you when to file.

Variations

Any change to the registration dossier — manufacturer, production site, composition, specifications, packaging and so on — is filed as a variation. If a production site is reorganised or renamed without changing its address or technology, the certificate is reissued: apply within one month; reissue takes 5 working days and carries no fee.

Labelling updates

Changes to the instructions for medical use or packaging texts are filed as variations to the registration dossier.

Portfolio tracking

We track expiry dates and regulatory changes across all your registered products.

Packaging or instructions change, in practice

Variations are reviewed within 50% of the registration period. In practice, a packaging artwork or instructions change takes 1–2 months, and changes involving laboratory testing take 3–6 months depending on complexity. Extending a certificate takes 135 days without laboratory testing, or 210 days if quality-related changes are made.

Fees

State fees are paid separately and are set by the Ministry of Health. We reply within one business day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

When should I apply for an extension?

We track your certificate's expiry and tell you when to file.

Which changes need a variation?

Any change to the registration dossier — manufacturer, production site, composition, specifications, packaging and so on — is filed as a variation. If a production site is reorganised or renamed without changing its address or technology, the certificate is reissued: apply within one month; reissue takes 5 working days and carries no fee.

Does a labelling change in Uzbekistan need approval?

Changes to the instructions for medical use or packaging texts are filed as variations to the registration dossier.

How long is a registration valid?

Registration certificates for medicines and medical devices are valid for five years. Import and production permits for dietary supplements, cosmetics and household chemicals are also issued for five years.
