Sports nutrition registration in Uzbekistan

Protein supplement registration, amino acids, pre-workout and recovery products. Depending on composition and dosage form, a sports nutrition product is treated either as a dietary supplement or as a specialised food for athletes.

What we handle

The right route for each product.

Classification

Depending on composition and dosage form, a sports nutrition product is treated either as a dietary supplement or as a specialised food for athletes.

Composition review

Active ingredients and dosages against permitted limits, and absence of prohibited substances.

Labelling and claims

Labelling and property claims reviewed for the Uzbek market.

Filing

Documents prepared and filed on your behalf.

Timeline

A written timeline, before we start.

15 working days
Statutory review, as a dietary supplement
2–3 months
Overall, as a dietary supplement

If the product is filed as a dietary supplement: the statutory review is 15 working days, in practice up to 30 working days. Laboratory testing before filing is not counted. Overall: 2–3 months.

Requirements and timelines depend on your product — contact us for details.

Fees

Fees depend on the product and pathway — request a quote.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is sports nutrition registered as a dietary supplement?

Depending on composition and dosage form, a sports nutrition product is treated either as a dietary supplement or as a specialised food for athletes.

How long does sports nutrition registration take?

If the product is filed as a dietary supplement: the statutory review is 15 working days, in practice up to 30 working days. Laboratory testing before filing is not counted. Overall: 2–3 months. Requirements and timelines depend on your product — contact us for details.

What do you check before submission?

Active ingredients and dosages against permitted limits, absence of prohibited substances, labelling and property claims.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the product and pathway — request a quote. State fees are paid separately and are set by the Ministry of Health.
